01

X-AXIS

The X-axis is the horizontal line.

  • It goes from left to right.
  • The X-coordinate tells us how far left or right a point is.
↕️
02

Y-AXIS

The Y-axis is the vertical line.

  • It goes from bottom to top.
  • The Y-coordinate tells us how far up or down a point is.
🎯
03

ORIGIN

The origin is where the X-axis and Y-axis meet.

(0, 0)

⭐ Think of the origin as the starting point of the coordinate plane.

👽
04

WHAT IS A COORDINATE?

Coordinates are written in the format:

(X,Y)
X comes FIRST.Y comes SECOND.
Example: (4,3)➡️ Move 4 units along X⬆️ Move 3 units along Y🎯 Point = (4,3)
🚀
05

HOW TO FIND A POINT

1Look at the X-coordinate and move left/right.
2Look at the Y-coordinate and move up/down.
3The two movements meet at the point.
Find (3,4)
➡️ Move 3 spaces right⬆️ Move 4 spaces up🎯 You reach the point (3,4)
